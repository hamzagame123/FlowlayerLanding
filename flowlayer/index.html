Memory Reconstruction // FlowLayer

Year 2748.

For over a century, humans have not driven their own vehicles.

After the Automation Accords of 2621, all transportation was handed over to artificial systems.

Steering wheels were removed. Pedals disappeared. Roads became optimized pathways for machines.

Driving was declared inefficient. Dangerous. Emotional.

And then it was forgotten.

⸻

In the present day, humanity lives almost entirely within neural virtual environments.

Consciousness is linked to immersive systems. Physical movement is optional. Real-world control is obsolete.

Very few remember what it felt like to grip a wheel.

To choose a road.

To get lost on purpose.

⸻

FlowLayer was created to restore a lost human experience.

Not transportation.

But intention.

Not efficiency.

But feeling.

⸻

Inside this simulation, you will not travel to arrive.

You will travel to remember.

Choose how you want to feel.

And drive.
